The Heat Tracker

Beat the heat. Save energy. Support Fairfield.

Fairfield spends about $1,000,000 a year on aircon. The Heat Tracker shows the live temperature inside public buildings, so you can head to a cooler (or warmer) community centre instead of running your own aircon.

Try it: drag the map, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap a building, then press Directions to see your walking route. Presenting? Tap the DEV button on the map to move the pretend user somewhere else.

    About this project

    The problem

    Fairfield wastes energy: about $1,000,000 a year on aircon, lights left on at night, and too few solar panels.

    Our solution

    The Heat Tracker shows which public building is coolest (or warmest), so people can go there instead of running their own aircon.

    Goal

    Lift visits to shopping centres and community centres by at least 10%, bringing money to local businesses.
